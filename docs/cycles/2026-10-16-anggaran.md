# Anggaran: budget per month, compared with the books

**Roadmap:** core accounting parity with Jurnal and Zahir (`docs/roadmap.md` §3). The owner asked on 2026-10-16 for Buku to be on par
with Jurnal and Zahir and better on the core accounting features, and delegated the decisions ("i trust your judgment").

## Context
Jurnal (*Anggaran* and its *Laporan Anggaran Laba Rugi*) and Zahir (*Anggaran*) both let a business plan its income and costs per month
and read the month against the plan. Buku has no budget at all, so the management pack and the owner's monthly conversation stop at
"this month vs last month". A firm that also advises its clients needs the plan next to the books.

Buku can do it better: the actuals are the ledger's own (every figure opens its Buku Besar through the reports), a budget can start from
last year's actual months with a growth percentage in one click, and every change is in Riwayat.

## Decisions
- **New table `Budget`** (additive migration): one amount per company, income or expense account and month, in minor units of the
  company's currency on the account's normal side. Planning data: never posted, never part of a statement, not subject to the period lock.
- **One page, *Anggaran*** (client menu, stage 3), two tabs:
  - *Anggaran vs realisasi* (default): the month and the financial year to date — budget, actual, difference (actual − budget) — per
    account with a budget or an actual, totals for income and expense, and the net result; *Realisasi ÷ anggaran* for the year to date.
    An unfavourable difference (income under, cost over) is marked "di luar rencana"; an account with actuals but no budget "tanpa
    anggaran". A group in one currency adds its companies; a group in several currencies asks for a company. Excel download.
  - *Atur anggaran*: one company's financial year, accounts × twelve months; *Setahun* spreads a yearly amount evenly (remainder on the
    last month); *Salin realisasi tahun lalu* fills every row from last year's months + a growth %; *Tambah akun* adds a row. Only changed
    cells are saved. Writers only (books.write); viewers read.
- **Integrity:** a company with a budget can't be removed (it is listed with its other data); deleting a client removes its budgets.
- **Riwayat:** one `BUDGET` event per save (cells set and cleared).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] *Atur anggaran* saves typed months; an emptied cell removes that month; *Setahun* and *Salin realisasi tahun lalu* fill the row(s).
- [ ] *Anggaran vs realisasi* shows the month and year to date per account, the totals and the net; actuals equal the Laba Rugi.
- [ ] Refused: a balance-sheet account, a month outside the financial year, an unreadable amount, another client's company.
- [ ] A group in several currencies asks for a company; the editor on a group asks for a company.
- [ ] The Excel download carries the table with difference formulas.
- [ ] Riwayat lists each save; a company with a budget can't be removed; deleting the client removes its budgets.
- [ ] Works at 390 px without page scroll sideways.

## Verify flows
1. Accountant, demo group, PT, August: *Atur anggaran* → 6120 *Setahun* 600.000.000 → 50.000.000 per month; 4100 March–August
   1.450.000.000 → *Simpan anggaran* → *Anggaran vs realisasi*: 4100 August 1.450.000.000 vs 1.505.720.721.
2. Excel download; Riwayat → Anggaran.
3. Edge: the group (combined) on *Atur anggaran* → pick a company. Error: a typed "1,5" → the save is refused with the cell named.

## Non-goals
- Balance-sheet budgets and cash budgets; budget versions or approval; department budgets.

## Gate re-openers
- **Schema migration** (additive: one table, its foreign keys and a month CHECK). Named here; approved under the owner's delegation.
- No dependency, no AI call, no auth change (books.write as other books work).
