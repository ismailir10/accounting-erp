## Context
How a bank line is decided at import, read from the code:
- **One choke point.** Every statement, from the import form, the Unggah inbox, an evidence review, an OCR draft and the demo seed, goes
  through the import pipeline. Each new line gets one suggestion, in rule 13's order: transfer matcher → rules → memory → financing /
  tax-payment hint → AI → simple guess.
- **One fixed test decides post vs review.** The method is TRANSFER, RULE or MEMORY, and the confidence is at least 0.9. A line that passes
  posts straight to its account. Every other line posts to 1999 in Review with the suggestion prefilled (accounting-rules 14).
- **What passes today:**
  - a transfer pair found (0.99), or an own transfer whose other half isn't imported yet (0.92);
  - a rule match (0.97);
  - a remembered choice (0.95).
  An ambiguous transfer (0.8), financing and tax-payment hints, AI and the simple guess always wait in Review.
- **The test is the same for every firm and every amount.** A Rp 2 bn line matched by a firm rule posts unseen, exactly like a Rp 6.500
  bank fee.
- **An earlier half gets moved.** When a new line pairs with a half imported earlier, that half is linked and moved onto 1199/1190 and
  set POSTED if it sat elsewhere.

Flux today (`scanLedger`, accounting-rules 22b):
- **Materiality** is 1 % of the baseline's average monthly P&L volume. Flip, dormant and duplicate findings use it too, and so does the
  floor of the adjustment candidates.
- **A P&L account is flagged** when its month differs from its baseline average by at least the materiality *and* by at least 50 % of
  that average.
- **One scan, many readers.** The same scan feeds:
  - the *Fluktuasi Laba Rugi tidak biasa* control on Tutup Buku;
  - *Melewati ambang* on Wawasan's Penggerak, and which movers may get *Susun dengan AI*;
  - the management pack's movers;
  - the AI close review's flux rows.
- **The hint on Wawasan** prints both fixed numbers.

Firm settings today:
- **The `Firm` row holds only what Buku admins set:** the AI token cap, the seat limit and a suspension.
- **Platform switches.** *Usulan AI* (key, model) and *Baca scan dengan AI* are platform-wide and Buku-admin only. Pengaturan kantor shows
  them read-only. So the *Umum* tab has no setting a firm can save yet.
- **History.** Riwayat perubahan is per client: `AuditEvent` needs a client and is append-only.

Who feels it:
- **The partner** who wants every line above, say, Rp 100 jt seen by a person before it reaches a report, or who doesn't trust a new
  junior's rules yet.
- **The accountant** whose flux control flags every Rp 3 jt swing on a large client, or misses a Rp 40 jt one on a small client.
- **The pilot's exit test:** "a higher share of rows posted with proof and no manual touch" only means something when the firm chose what
  may post.

Rillet's dial has four modes (high confidence, rule, medium confidence, never) plus an amount cap. Buku's deterministic sources are
already that split: transfer = proven by the pair, rule = the firm's words, memory = the accountant's own past decisions.

## Decisions
- **Three switches and one cap, on the firm.**
  - The switches: *Transfer antar rekening*, *Aturan* and *Pilihan yang diingat*.
  - The cap: *Batas nominal* in whole Rupiah, empty = no cap.
  - New `Firm` columns, all with defaults equal to today: switches on, no cap. Every existing firm, the demo and every new trial behave
    exactly as now, with no backfill.
  - Per firm, not per client, as the roadmap says.
- **The dial can only send more to Review, never less.**
  - A line posts directly only when four things hold: its method is TRANSFER, RULE or MEMORY; its confidence is ≥ 0.9 (the threshold stays
    a code constant); its source's switch is on; and its absolute amount is at or below the cap.
  - AI, the simple guess and the financing / tax-payment hints never post, whatever the settings (invariant 4, rule 14). The card shows
    *Usulan AI* as a fixed line, "selalu menunggu persetujuan", with no switch.
- **A held line is an ordinary Review line.**
  - Status NEEDS_REVIEW on 1999, suggested account and tax tag as classified. Method and confidence are kept, so it reads "Aturan 97 %",
    and the reason says why it waits:
    - over the cap: "Di atas batas otomasi (Rp 100.000.000). <the classifier's reason>";
    - source off: "Otomasi aturan dimatikan di Pengaturan kantor. <reason>" (likewise *transfer*, *pilihan yang diingat*).
  - It shows in Review and in Kotak persetujuan (kind *Klasifikasi bank*), and Kotak's *Setujui* = Review's accept, unchanged.
  - It is never in *Setujui N usulan yakin*, because that bulk stays AI-only (`isSureSuggestion`). The point of holding a line is that a
    person looks at it. Review's existing same-merchant accept still applies.
- **Transfers held by the dial:**
  - **A pair inside one import** is still linked: a link is a fact, not a posting. Both halves wait in Review with 1199/1190 suggested.
    Review's accept on 1199/1190 keeps the pair, and any other account releases it, as today.
  - **A pair with a half imported earlier.** The earlier half is never moved or re-posted.
    - Still in Review: it is linked, and its suggestion becomes the transfer account. Nothing posts.
    - Already on the transfer account: it is linked.
    - Posted to another account: it is left unlinked and untouched, and the new line's reason names it. The clearing control then
      shows the open half, as for any unpaired transfer.
- **New imports only.** Changing the dial never re-posts, re-classifies or moves an existing line. Turning a switch on doesn't post the
  lines already waiting, and turning it off doesn't pull back lines already posted. The card says so. A line already in Review is decided
  there.
- ***Ambang fluktuasi*: two firm numbers, used by the flux scan only.**
  - **Selisih minimal (Rp):** empty = none.
  - **Perubahan minimal (%):** default 50, whole percent, 1–1000.
  - A P&L account is flagged when |Δ| ≥ max(1 % materiality, Selisih minimal) and |Δ| ≥ Perubahan minimal % of its average. The other
    conditions are unchanged: at least 2 baseline months, and 7190/7200 exempt.
  - The 1 % volume materiality stays fixed. It also scales flip, dormant, duplicates and the adjustment-candidate floor, which the roadmap
    item doesn't name. The Rp floor is the firm's materiality in Rupiah, and the % is the change an accountant means by *fluktuasi*.
  - **Rupiah only.** The Rp floor applies to Rupiah companies. A company kept in another currency uses the % and the computed materiality,
    because a Rupiah figure is never compared with another currency's minor units without a rate (rule 3), and the hint says so.
- **One reader, so every page agrees.** The scan reads the firm's thresholds itself, so the control, Wawasan, the management pack, the AI
  explanation's eligibility and the AI close review always flag the same accounts.
  - An approved explanation stays valid: its staleness is tested on the account's series, not on the flag.
  - Changing a threshold changes which accounts the AI close review sends, so a cached review no longer matches. A new review costs a
    call only when someone asks for it.
- **Admins only, read-only for everyone else.**
  - Saving needs the existing `org.settings` capability (Pemilik, Admin). It is refused while the organisation is read-only or in a
    support session, as every write is. No change to the permission matrix.
  - Akuntan and Peninjau see the current dial and thresholds, with no controls.
- **Every change is in Riwayat perubahan.**
  - One save that changes something writes a new `AUTOMATION` audit event ("Otomasi kantor") into the Riwayat perubahan of every client of
    the firm, in the same transaction as the change.
  - Each event holds before and after, with amounts as strings. Its summary is a one-line diff, for example "Aturan: langsung dicatat →
    ke Review; Batas nominal: tanpa batas → Rp 100.000.000".
  - Riwayat is per client because that is where an accountant asks "why did this line wait?". The card shows the last change (date and who)
    from those events.
  - A save that changes nothing writes nothing, and *Simpan* stays disabled.
- **The import result says what the dial held.** Next to "langsung dijurnal / perlu review", a line reads "N ditahan Otomasi kantor
  (batas nominal, aturan dimatikan …)". It shows only when N > 0.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] With default settings, an import posts and holds exactly the lines it does today. The demo seeds identically and
      `verify:books` is ALL PASS. The investor walk and the existing e2e pass unchanged.
- [ ] With *Aturan* off, a line a rule matches waits in Review on 1999 with the rule's account and tax tag suggested, "Aturan 97 %", and
      the reason "Otomasi aturan dimatikan…". The same holds for *Pilihan yang diingat* and for *Transfer antar rekening*, where a pair
      inside one import stays linked.
- [ ] With a *Batas nominal*, a deterministic line whose absolute amount is above it waits in Review with "Di atas batas otomasi (Rp …)".
      A line equal to the cap posts.
- [ ] AI and simple-guess lines never post, with every switch on and no cap (existing behaviour, asserted again).
- [ ] A held transfer whose other half was imported earlier never moves or re-posts that half:
      - in Review, it is linked and gets the transfer suggestion;
      - on the transfer account, it is linked;
      - elsewhere, it is untouched and named in the reason.
- [ ] Changing the dial changes no existing line: Jurnal Umum, Review and every report read the same before and after a save.
- [ ] Held lines show in Kotak persetujuan (*Klasifikasi bank*), are never in *Setujui N usulan yakin*, and accept like any Review line.
- [ ] The import result shows "N ditahan Otomasi kantor" when the dial held lines.
- [ ] *Ambang fluktuasi*:
      - Perubahan minimal 60 % unflags PT Jasa Kreatif Digital's July 6170 (+52 %), on Wawasan and on the Tutup Buku flux control alike.
      - Selisih minimal Rp 6.000.000 unflags it too (Δ Rp 5.983.334).

      - A non-Rupiah company ignores the Rp floor.
- [ ] Wawasan's threshold hint states the firm's numbers, and where to change them.
- [ ] Pemilik and Admin can save the card. Akuntan and Peninjau see it read-only, and the action refuses them with "Hanya admin kantor
      yang dapat mengubah ini.". Invalid input is refused with a field message and nothing is saved.
- [ ] Each save that changes something adds one "Otomasi kantor" event, with before and after, to every client's Riwayat perubahan, and the

- [ ] Works at 390 px without sideways page scroll.
- [ ] Deck claims to review in ship: `kantor.html` slide 07 *Review* ("Transfer yang terbukti, aturan dan memori klien bisa dibukukan

## Non-goals
- A dial per client, per bank account or per rule; a confidence slider (0.9 stays a code constant); turning the financing hints or the
  simple guess into posting sources.
- Re-running classification or posting the lines already in Review when a switch turns on; pulling back posted lines when it turns off.
- Bulk *Setujui* for held lines (Review's same-merchant accept already exists).
- Firm-level settings for the 1 % materiality, the 3-month baseline, flip, dormant or duplicate scans.
- Flux as a close step (explanations clearing or gating the flux control on Tutup Buku; roadmap item 4's flow); *Instruksi kantor*
  (item 5); approval routing by source and amount (Phase 3 item 2).
- A firm-wide Riwayat page; making `AuditEvent.clientId` optional.
- Stamping a closed month's controls with the thresholds in force when it locked.

## Assumptions
- **The cap compares the line's absolute amount in whole Rupiah.** Statements are Rupiah-only (the pipeline refuses others), so the cap is
  exact. The input takes at most 15 digits, as the import does.
- **A closed month is not re-gated.** Controls are computed when read, so a closed month's Tutup Buku shows the flux control under the
  current thresholds, as it does after any change to the code's rules. The lock doesn't depend on them once taken.
- **One event per client per save is cheap.** A firm holds tens of clients, not thousands. A firm with no clients records no event, and the
  card then shows no "Terakhir diubah". There is nothing the dial could have affected.
- **Clients added later** start their Riwayat without earlier dial changes. The card always shows the current settings.
- **Accepting a held RULE or MEMORY line unchanged** learns Memory as any Review accept does. That is harmless, because rule 13's order
  puts rules before memory.
- **Accounting-rules 14 and 22b** are updated in the same commits: stricter only, and AI stays review-only. No ADR is needed: the change
  is reversible (switch it back) and follows the roadmap item.
- **Open for the owner** (the recommended answer is the one written above):
  1. **Which % the firm sets.** The recommendation is the flux % (change against the account's average), with the 1 % volume materiality
     kept and floored by the Rp minimum. The alternative is the materiality % itself, which also moves flip, dormant and duplicates.
  2. **Where the history lives.** The recommendation is one event per client. The alternative is a firm-level history, a migration making
     `AuditEvent.clientId` nullable.
  3. **The transfer switch.** The recommendation is one switch for pairs and unpaired own transfers alike. The alternative is two switches
     (0.99 vs 0.92), closer to Rillet's high/medium.

## Gate re-openers
- **Migration (planned):** additive `Firm` columns, with defaults equal to today and no backfill:
  - three switches (boolean, default true);
  - the cap (bigint Rupiah, nullable, CHECK > 0);
  - the flux floor (bigint Rupiah, nullable, CHECK > 0);
  - the flux percent (int, default 50, CHECK 1–1000).
  It goes under `prisma/migrations/**`, a sensitive path.
- **Accounting invariant text changes** (rule 14: "…and the firm's dial allows it, at or below its cap"; rule 22b: the firm's thresholds).
  They are stricter only. Invariant 4 is unchanged.
- **Sensitive paths touched:**
  - `lib/settings/**`: a new module that reads, validates and saves the dial and thresholds and writes the audit events;
  - `app/*actions.ts`: a new `app/firm-settings-actions.ts` guarded by `requireCapability("org.settings")`, kept apart from
    `settings-actions.ts`, whose actions are Buku-admin only;
  - `prisma/migrations/**`.
  The permission matrix is unchanged.
- **Reports:** no number moves with default settings (`verify:books` ALL PASS). A non-default flux threshold changes which movers the
  management pack lists and which accounts Wawasan marks, by design.
- **None of the rest:** no dependency, no real AI call (MockProvider only), no production write.

If the transfer earlier-half rules turn out to need a new link state or column, that is a second migration: stop and ask.
